Privacy Policy

The Black Farm: Horror · Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game The Black Farm: Horror (the "Game", Windows package MaazKhawaja.TheBlackFarmHorror, Android package com.blackfarm.game) handles your information. The Game is developed by Maaz Khawaja (the "Developer").

1. Information we collect

The Game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no third-party tracking tools. The Game does not use the internet.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your settings and progress locally on your device (for example volume, graphics quality, the nights you have reached, grades, collected items and your in-game journal). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the Developer. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

3. Third-party services

The Game is built with the Godot Engine and does not include any third-party SDKs that collect data. The Game is distributed through the Microsoft Store and Google Play. Their handling of data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is a horror game intended for players aged 16 and over. Because the Game collects no personal data, no personal information from children is collected.

5. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about your rights under the GDPR or other laws, please contact us.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
